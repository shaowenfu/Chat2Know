和 AI 聊着学，选中一段，一下记进你的知识导图 Learn by chatting with AI. Select a line, and it lands on your knowledge map.

Chat2Know 是一个浏览器插件。记笔记只要一次点击，放到导图的哪里由它来定；你在一个地方绕圈子时，它在侧栏轻声提醒你可能卡在哪、怎么问更好。 Chat2Know is a browser extension. Recording a note takes one click, and it decides where on your map the note belongs. When you start going in circles, it points out in the side panel where you may be stuck and how to ask better.

记下Note 保护原文Keep words 提问Ask 适用于幂指函数 y = u(x)v(x)：先两边取对数，再对两边求导，最后乘回 y。 It works for functions like y = u(x)v(x): take logs of both sides, then differentiate both sides, and multiply back by y.

例如 y = xx，ln y = x ln x，于是 y′ = xx(ln x + 1)。 For y = xx: ln y = x ln x, so y′ = xx(ln x + 1).

要花钱吗？Does it cost anything?

插件免费、开源（AGPL-3.0）。模型调用按你自己的服务商计费，用你自己的 Key；不配模型也能记笔记。The extension is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). Model calls are billed by your own provider, on your own key; recording works without a model.

支持 Safari 或 Firefox 吗？Does it work in Safari or Firefox?

暂时不支持。在 Mac 上可以装 Chrome、Edge、Brave 或 Arc 来用，和 Safari 并存，不影响原来的浏览器。Not yet. On a Mac, install Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc alongside Safari.

它会替我做题或者给我打分吗？Will it quiz me or grade me?

不会。它不出题、不打分、不排复习计划。它只帮你把学到的记清楚，在你卡住时提醒一句；报告也只在你点了之后才写。No. No quizzes, no scores, no review schedules. It keeps what you learn in order and speaks up when you seem stuck; a report is written only when you ask.

提问会被上传吗？Are my questions uploaded?

提问只存在你的浏览器里。每 5 个问题检查一次时，最近的提问会发给你配置的模型；不想要可以在设置里关掉提问记录，记笔记照常可用。Your questions are stored only in your browser. For the check every 5 questions, the latest ones are sent to the model you configured; turn question recording off in settings if you prefer, and recording notes still works.

支持哪些模型？Which models work?

任意兼容 OpenAI 格式的接口：填接口地址、模型名和 Key 即可，比如 DeepSeek、OpenAI、Kimi。嵌入模型可选，能让归位更准。Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: enter its base URL, model and key, such as DeepSeek, OpenAI or Kimi. An embedding model is optional and makes placement more accurate.
