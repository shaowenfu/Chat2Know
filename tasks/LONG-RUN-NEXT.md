- S1: a third item type, `quote`, holds the selection word for word (`CapturedSelection.words`: the browser's text with line breaks kept, or the Markdown when a formula or code block is selected, since KaTeX's rendered text doubles). - S2: 提问 on the selection bar opens `buildAskWindow` (artboard 14) beside the selection: the selection as a quote, the question, the answer as it streams, then ＋ 记到导图 (a statement: `answerStatement` makes it one line with `$…$` formulas; placement compresses it), 保护原文 (the answer word for word) and 复制; follow-ups replay the earlier turns. The window can be dragged by its header, closes on × or Esc, and keeps the page from taking its keys. Nothing of it is stored but the questions (through the same gate as the site's questions; S3 moves them to the open map). Streaming: one runtime port per question (`ASK_PORT`, `AskStart` / `AskEvent`); the background builds `askPrompt` (both languages: the selection, its context, the page title, then the turns) and reads `LlmClient.stream` (server-sent deltas); closing the port aborts the request. Answers render through `answerHtml` (escaped text, a little Markdown, KaTeX as MathML, so no fonts or styles are needed on the page). Root cause found on the way: the KaTeX bundle carries a literal U+FFFF, and Chrome refuses to load a content script holding a Unicode noncharacter ("isn't UTF-8 encoded") without any error on the page; a build plugin in `wxt.config.ts` writes noncharacters back as escapes. The site script grows from 59 KB to 325 KB with KaTeX. Screenshot `page-ask.png` matches artboard 14.
- S3: A question (site or floating window, through one gate: the sites ticked, recording on) is stored with the map open at the time (`Ask.mapId`, `via`); none when no map is open. The card (`StuckCard.vue`, artboard 15) sits above the tree: a title made from the kind and the topic, the explanation, "可以这样问" with copy, "看看我问过的" (the evidence questions), "不用". The full page shows only the orange outline. Removed: tagging, the three signals, prerequisites, root causes, 整理进来, 出道题, the `alarms` permission and the conversation-idle timer; DB version 2 drops `askTags`, `prereqEdges`, `gaps` and adds `stuckChecks` and the `mapId` index (an upgrade test keeps v1 maps and questions).
